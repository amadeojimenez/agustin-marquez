Paintings

Painting is where my artistic journey began—a practice that continues to be a profound medium of personal expression. Through brushstrokes and color, I explore emotions and concepts that often transcend verbal articulation.

My approach to painting is intuitive and experimental, allowing the canvas to become a space for dialogue between conscious technique and subconscious expression. I work primarily with oils and acrylics, though I frequently incorporate mixed media elements to achieve textural depth and visual complexity.

The works displayed here represent various periods of my artistic development, from more traditional landscape studies to abstract explorations of color and form. Each painting carries with it a piece of my perception and emotional landscape at the time of its creation.
