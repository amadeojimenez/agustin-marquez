About Agustin

Born in Buenos Aires, Argentina, I've spent the last fifteen years exploring the world through the lens of my camera and the stroke of my brush. My journey as a visual artist began with traditional photography but quickly expanded to include videography and painting as I sought new ways to express my artistic vision.

My work is deeply influenced by my travels across South America, Europe, and Africa, where I've documented both natural landscapes and human stories. I believe that art has the power to transcend cultural boundaries and communicate universal truths about our shared humanity and relationship with the natural world.

With a formal background in Fine Arts from the University of Buenos Aires and a Master's in Digital Media from the London School of Arts, I combine traditional techniques with contemporary approaches. My pieces have been exhibited in galleries across Argentina, Spain, and the United States, and my video work has been featured in several international film festivals.
